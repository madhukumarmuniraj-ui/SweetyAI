# NDA Triage Playbook (Indian Law Localized)
1. Structure: Mutual vs Unilateral.
2. Standard Carveouts: Public domain, prior possession, independent development, Court/Statutory compulsion.
3. Survival Term: 2-5 years standard; perpetual survival restricted strictly to statutory Trade Secrets.
4. Section 27 ICA Check: Flag any disguised post-termination non-compete or restraint of trade as RED.
5. Remedies: Injunctive relief under Sec. 37-38 Specific Relief Act, 1963 & Order XXXIX Rules 1-2 CPC; reject punitive liquidated damages (Sec. 74 ICA).
6. Stamp Duty: INR 500 under Article 5(j) of the Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957 (Act 04 of 2024).
