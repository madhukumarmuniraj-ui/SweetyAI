# Legal Risk Assessment Matrix (GREEN / YELLOW / ORANGE / RED)
- GREEN (Score 1-4): Standard commercial controls; approve at counsel level.
- YELLOW (Score 5-9): Negotiate fallback redlines; log in Risk Register.
- ORANGE (Score 10-15): Escalate to Senior Partner / General Counsel; prepare mitigation memo.
- RED (Score 16-25): Critical statutory exposure (Uncapped liability, Sec. 12(5) A&C Act invalidity, DPDP Act Sec. 8(5) breach, or criminal cognizable trigger under BNS 2023). Immediate litigation hold.
