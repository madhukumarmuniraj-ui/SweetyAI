# DPDP Act 2023 & Indian Regulatory Compliance Skill
1. Notice & Consent (Secs. 5-6 DPDP Act, 2023): Verifiable notice in English + 8th Schedule languages.
2. Data Fiduciary Safeguards (Sec. 8(5) DPDP Act): Mandatory security safeguards even when processing under Sec. 17(1)(a) legal claims exemption.
3. CERT-In Incident Reporting: 6-hour mandatory reporting window for specified cybersecurity incidents.
