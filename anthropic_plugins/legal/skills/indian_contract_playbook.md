# INDIAN COMMERCIAL CONTRACT REVIEW & REDLINE PLAYBOOK

## 1. Limitation of Liability (Sections 73 & 74, Indian Contract Act, 1872)
- **Standard:** Mutual cap at 100% of preceding 12 months' fees. Exclude indirect, consequential, and remote damages under Section 73 ICA.
- **Red Flag:** Uncapped liability or punitive penalty clauses violating *Kailash Nath Associates v. DDA*, (2015) 4 SCC 136.
- **Redline Fallback:** "Aggregate liability of either Party shall not exceed 100% of fees paid in the 12 months preceding the cause of action. Neither Party shall be liable for indirect or consequential losses under Section 73 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872."

## 2. Arbitration & Dispute Resolution (Arbitration & Conciliation Act, 1996)
- **Standard:** Mutual appointment of Sole Arbitrator or institutional rules (Arbitration & Conciliation Centre - Bengaluru). Exclusive seat and venue at Bengaluru, Karnataka.
- **Red Flag:** Unilateral appointment of Sole Arbitrator or narrow curated panel violating Section 12(5) & Seventh Schedule (*Perkins Eastman v. HSCC*, (2020) 20 SCC 760; *CORE v. ECI SPIC*, 2024 SCC OnLine SC 3219).
- **Redline Fallback:** "All disputes shall be resolved by a Sole Arbitrator appointed mutually by the Parties, failing which in accordance with the Rules of the Arbitration & Conciliation Centre, Bengaluru. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Bengaluru, Karnataka."

## 3. Bar Against Shortened Limitation (Section 28, Indian Contract Act, 1872)
- **Standard:** Claims governed by the Limitation Act, 1963 (3 years under Article 55 / 113).
- **Red Flag:** Any clause extinguishing rights or barring claims if not notified/filed within 30/60/90 days (Void under Section 28(a)/(b) ICA).

## 4. Restraint of Trade & Non-Compete (Section 27, Indian Contract Act, 1872)
- **Standard:** Post-termination survival restricted to Confidentiality and Non-Solicitation of direct personnel/clients.
- **Red Flag:** Post-termination non-compete restricting lawful profession or trade (*Percept D'Mark v. Zaheer Khan*, (2006) 4 SCC 227).

## 5. Specific Performance & Termination (Sections 14(d) & 20, Specific Relief Act, 1963)
- **Standard:** 30-day termination for convenience + 30-day written cure notice prior to exercising substituted performance under Section 20 SRA.

## 6. Stamp Duty Compliance (Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957)
- **Standard:** Explicit stamp duty allocation under the Schedule to the Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957 to prevent impounding and 10x penalty under Sections 33 and 34.
